# Privacy & compliance notes

**Yeh legal salah nahi hai.** Bharat mein commercial calls ke liye TRAI/TCCCPR, DLT registration, DND/NDNC aur calling-hours ke niyam lagte hain — apne vakil/telecom provider se confirm karein.

## Ab app kya karta hai
- Sirf us customer ko campaign mein rakhta hai jiski **calling sahmati** darj hai; sahmati ki tareekh aur har badlav `consent_log` mein.
- Dial se theek pehle sahmati dobara jaanchta hai; **opt-out** ek tap mein (customer profile).
- Calls sirf 9:00-21:00; ek campaign mein ek customer ko ek hi baar; 24 ghante mein no-answer/reject ko dobara nahi.
- AI sahayak greeting mein khud ko AI batata hai. Call recording / microphone / call-log permission nahi maangi jaati.
- Data phone par; PIN hash ke roop mein encrypted storage mein; cloud backup sirf aap chalu karein.

## Jo app nahi karta (aapko karna hoga)
- **DND/NDNC registry check** — app online registry se nahi milata. Apni list pehle scrub karein.
- DLT registration / approved headers — SIM par normal call ke liye ye aapke operator ke niyam hain.
- Backup file/CSV mein ph. numbers hote hain — surakshit rakhein.
